## Welcome to my guestbook!

Please take a moment to sign it. Let me know where you're visiting from, what browser you're using, and whether the page loaded quickly on your connection.

This one is real. It is not a form that posts to `guestbook.pl` and appends to a flat file on a server in someone's basement — it's [giscus](https://giscus.app), which stores entries as GitHub Discussions on this repo. You sign in with GitHub, your entry lives in a place I control, and it is considerably harder to fill with casino spam than the 1996 version was.

### Guestbook rules

1. No flaming.
2. No advertising.
3. No asking me to check out your Angelfire page. I will check out your Angelfire page.
4. Tell me if something here is broken. Genuinely — these posts went five years without an edit, and I would much rather hear about a wrong command than not.

> Found an actual mistake in a post? A [GitHub issue](https://github.com/samuelzamvil/samuelzamvil.github.io/issues/new) or a pull request is the fastest route to getting it fixed.
